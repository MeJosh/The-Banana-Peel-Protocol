## Character guide

Character interactions and Storyteller advice.

### Unchanged or minor adjustments

**Function as normal:** Gossip, Cannibal, Farmer.

**Reversed information:** Shugenja, Town Crier, Seamstress, Artist.

### Changed roles and interactions

**Investigator:** Learns two players who cannot be the named Minion. Use this to rule out Witch, Organ Grinder, or Scarlet Woman candidates. You can show an actual Minion with a different Minion name that town can work out is absent. Give them a useful puzzle.

**Librarian:** With only one Outsider intended in play, learns two players who are not the Hermit.

**Empath:** Acts like a reusable Seamstress for their living neighbors: learning a 1 means they are the same alignment; 0 or 2 means different alignments.

**Dreamer:** Learns two characters the chosen player is not. Showing Hermit rules out a Hermit candidate; showing an evil character likely in play rules out that Demon or Minion. Use the choices to challenge an evil bluff or build trust by disproving a good player's bluff.

**Savant:** Both statements must be false, making this a stronger ability than usual. Balance with weaker statements or a deliberately unhelpful one, such as "You are the Vortox."

**Sage:** If killed by the Demon, learns two players who are not the Demon: effectively a late-game Knight. A Gossip death can also help build trust in the Gossip if town does not suspect an Assassin.

### Hermit

**Hermit:** Has a Townsfolk token but is an Outsider, so the Vortox does not affect their information. True information can hint that they are the Hermit. Use that sparingly; as with the Drunk, their information should generally hinder or confuse good.
