# Banana Peel Protocol

## Introduction

Banana Peel Protocol is a script designed as a release feature for the Hermit. The premise of the script is "what if there is always exactly one Outsider in play—the Hermit?" Because the Hermit has the Drunk's ability, each Townsfolk doesn't know for sure whether they are the Hermit. Because of this, each Townsfolk upon dying must make a Klutz pick, with only the _real_ Hermit's pick actually having any impact on the game. Choose wisely, and be careful not to slip!

## Evil team

Another feature of the script is that it's a solo Demon script featuring the Vortox! This means that Townsfolk _know_ that their information must be incorrect. For some characters, such as the Artist or the Shugenja, this is easily reversible and has no effect. For other characters, such as the Sage or Dreamer, it has massive effects on the character's role and power. More details on Townsfolk interactions follow in the character guide.

In addition to the Demon, the evil team features Minions designed to add chaos and confusion to town's goal: "solve the Hermit puzzle." The Witch, Assassin, and Organ Grinder cause unexpected or additional deaths, forcing players to make on-the-spot Klutz picks, while the Scarlet Woman acts as an extra buffer of protection for the fragile, exposed Vortox. Be warned that in lower Minion count games, Minions might want to be careful about using their abilities, lest they give town too much information early on about the composition of the evil team!
