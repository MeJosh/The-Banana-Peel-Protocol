## Character guide

A quick breakdown of how the Townsfolk function on this script, with advice for the Storyteller.

### Unchanged or minor adjustments

The following characters are functionally unchanged or only have minor adjustments to how they would act in any other Clocktower game:

**Shugenja:** Receives the opposite direction as they would normally.

**Town Crier:** Receives the opposite information as they would normally.

**Gossip:** Functions identically to regular games.

**Seamstress:** Receives reversed information.

**Artist:** Receives the opposite information as they would normally.

**Cannibal:** Functions normally—see associated Townsfolk abilities for detailed interactions.

**Farmer:** Functions normally.

### Changed roles and interactions

Several characters have slightly different roles than they would in most games of Clocktower:

**Investigator:** Learns two players who CANNOT be a specific Minion. This is often useful to eliminate two people as candidates for a "loud" Minion, such as a Witch or Organ Grinder, or rule out two people as a Scarlet Woman candidate. You can also show an actual Minion player, but a Minion that town might be able to figure out is not in play. It's important to remember the Investigator is a Townsfolk, so we want to try and make this an interesting puzzle for them to solve and contribute to.

**Librarian:** As there is supposed to be only one Outsider in play, the use of the Librarian is to learn two players who are NOT the Hermit.

**Empath:** The Empath is a slightly powered-down version of its normal role. Often acting as a "reusable Seamstress" for its neighbors, either learning a 1 indicating that their neighbors are the same alignment, or a 0/2 which shows that they are not.

**Dreamer:** The Dreamer is probably the most changed functionally from the base game. The Dreamer must learn two characters that a player is not. I often show the Dreamer _either_ the Hermit (eliminating that player as a Hermit candidate, and knowing they can trust their information), or an evil character that is likely in play (eliminating the player as a Demon or Minion candidate). You can also use it to allow the Dreamer to push on an evil bluff, or help them build trust by disproving a bluff from a good player.

**Savant:** The Savant learns two statements that _must_ be false. This is generally a strict upgrade to the Savant, so don't be afraid to water down the statements a little bit to compensate, or give one statement that is functionally useless (e.g., "You are the Vortox") to balance.

**Sage:** The Sage learns two players that are not the Demon if their ability triggers. A Knight later in the game can still be incredibly powerful. You can also kill them with a Gossip if town doesn't believe it to be an Assassin game to help build trust with the Gossip.

### Hermit

**Hermit:** While not technically a Townsfolk, they will have a Townsfolk token. The important thing to remember is the Hermit is _not_ impacted by the Vortox's ability. While you often will want to give them false information, a Hermit learning that some of their information is true can potentially be a clue to knowing that they are the Hermit. Remember the Hermit is an Outsider, so, similar to the Drunk in regular games, use this sparingly, and generally in places that will hinder or confuse the good team.
