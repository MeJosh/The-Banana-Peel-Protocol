# Banana Peel Protocol

## Introduction

The Banana Peel Protocol is a script that was designed as a release feature of the Hermit. The premise of the script is "what if there is always exactly one outsider in play - the Hermit". Because the Hermit has the Drunk's ability, each townsfolk doesn't know for sure if they are / are not the Hermit, so because of this each Townsfolk upon dying must make a Klutz pick, with only the _real_ Hermit's pick actually having any impact on the game. Chose wisely, and be careful not to slip!

## Evil Team

Another feature of the script is that it's a solo demon script featuring the Vortox! This means that townsfolk _know_ that their information must be incorrect. For some characters, such as the Artist or the Shugenja, this is easily reversable and has no effect. For other cahracters such as the Sage or Dreamer, it has massive changed on the characters role / power. More details on townsfolk interactions below.

In addition to the Demon, the evil team features Minions designed to add chaos and confusion to Town's goal "solve the Hermit puzzle". The Witch, Assassin, and Organ Grinder cause unexpected or additional deaths - forcing players to make on the spot Klutz picks, while the Scarlet Woman acting as an extra buffer of protection for the fragile, exposed Vortox. Be warned that in lower Minion count games, Minions might want to be careful about using their abilities - less they give town too much information early on about the composition of the evil team!

## Townsfolk

A quick breakdown / guide of how the Townsfolk function on this script:

The following characters are functionally unchanged or only have minor adjustments to how they would act in any other clocktower game:

**Shugenja:** Receives the opposite direction as they would normally.

**Town Crier:** Receives the opposite information as they would normally.

**Gossip:** Functions identically to regular games.

**Seamstress:** Receives reversed information.

**Arist:** Receives the opposite information as they would normally.

**Cannibal:** Functions normally - see associated townsfolk abilities for detailed interactions.

**Farmer:** Functions normally.

Then there are several characters who have slightly different roles then they would in most games of Clocktower:

**Investigator:** Learns two players who CANNOT be a specific minion. This is often useful to eliminate two people as candidates of a "loud" minion, such as a Witch or Organ Grinder, or rule out two people as a Scarlet Woman candidate. You can also show an actual minion player, but a minion that Town might be able to figure out is not in play. It's important to remember the Investigator is a Townsfolk, so we want to try and make this an interesting puzzle for them to solve and contribute.

**Librarian:** As there is supposed to be only one outsider in play, the use of the Librarian is to learn two players who are NOT the Hermit.

**Empath:** Empath is a slightly powered down version of it's normal role. Often acting as a "re-usable Seamstress" for it's neighbors, either learning a "1" indicating that their neighbors are the same alignment, or a 0/2 which shows that they are not.

**Dreamer:** The Dreamer is probably the most changed functionally from the base game. The Dreamer must learn two characters that a player is not. I often show the dreamer_either_ the Hermit (eliminating that player as a Hermit candidate, and knowing they can trust their information), or an Evil that is likely in play (eliminating the Player as a Demon / Minion candidate). You can also use it to allow the Dreamer to push on an evil bluff, or help them build trust by disproving a bluff from a good player

**Savant:** The Savant learns two statements that _must_ be false. This is generally a strict upgrade to the Savant, so don't be afraid to water down the statements a little bit to compensate, or give one statement that is functionally useless (e.x. "You are the Vortox") to balance.

**Sage:** The Sage learns two players that are not the Demon if their ability triggers. A Knight later on the game can still be incredibly powerful. You can also kill them to a Gossip if town doesn't believe it to be an Assassin game to help build trust with the Gossip.

**Hermit:** While not technically a Townsfolk, they will have a townsfolk token. The important thing to remember is the Hermit is _not_ impacted by the Vortox's ability. While you often will want to give them false information, a Hermit learning that some of their information is true can potentially be a clue to knowing that they are the Hermit. Remember the Hermit is an outsider, so similar to the Drunk in regular games use this sparingly, and generally in places that will hinder / confuse the good team.
